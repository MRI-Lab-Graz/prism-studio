OverviewCross-sectional study on the relationship between cardiorespiratory fitness (VO2max) and general cognitive ability (IQ) in healthy young adults.
Study designCross-sectional, single session, one lab visit.
RecruitmentConvenience sample recruited via university mailing lists and notice boards. Data collection: January to June 2026.
EligibilityInclusion: age 18 to 35, normal or corrected-to-normal vision, able to run on a treadmill. Exclusion: cardiovascular disease, neurological or psychiatric disorder.
ProcedureParticipants complete an IQ test (about 60 minutes), then a graded treadmill test to exhaustion with spiroergometry to determine VO2max (about 30 minutes).
