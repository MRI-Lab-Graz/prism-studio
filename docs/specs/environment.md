# Environment Specification

This page describes the PRISM environment modality at a high level.

## Scope

The `environment` modality enriches a session with its surroundings (weather, air quality, light, noise, room sensors), so you can ask questions such as temperature versus reaction time, season, or heat days. Like `biosample`, every column is a term of the registry (or has `Description` and `Units`).

## Position in PRISM

- **PRISM (model)** defines the structure and metadata expectations for environment sidecars and tables.
- **PRISM Studio (software)** executes environment workflows (generation, validation runs, and exports where applicable).

## Typical Artifacts

- Environment TSV tables with one column per quantity.
- Matching JSON sidecars with the blocks `Technical` (`Source`, resolutions), `Study` (`EnvironmentName`) and `Metadata`, plus one definition per column.
- Files are named `sub-<label>[_ses-<label>]_recording-<label>_environment.tsv` (`.tsv.gz` is also accepted) with a matching `_environment.json` sidecar. The `recording` entity is required and alphanumeric; there is no `task` entity. `Study.EnvironmentName` equals the `recording` label.

## Window and Statistic

A column may say over which time it was computed. `Window` has `Start` and `End`, ISO 8601 durations counted back from the session start (`PT0S` is the session itself); `Statistic` is one of `value`, `mean`, `min`, `max`, `sum`, `count`. For example `temp_max_3d` with `Window` `{"Start": "P3D", "End": "PT0S"}` and `Statistic` `max` is the highest temperature in the three days up to the session.

## Validation

Environment files are validated by PRISM Studio/CLI against the environment schema used by your selected schema version.

## Notes

Do not store exact location or raw timestamps unless your governance and privacy policy explicitly allow it.
