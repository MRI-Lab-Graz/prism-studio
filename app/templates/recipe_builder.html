{% call help_panel('How formulas and best-of work', 'info', false, 'fas fa-circle-question', 'py-2', 'mb-3') %}

A formula calculates a score from items. Write each item in curly brackets, e.g. {weight_kg}/{height_m}**2. Use + - * / % ** (% is the remainder, ** is "to the power of"), brackets, comparisons < <= > >= == != with and / or (for ifelse conditions), and the functions min max sum abs round sqrt exp log ifelse.

Empty rule: if any item the score lists is empty, the score is empty.

Best of several items: add one entry per group of trials (a name, max or min, and the trial items). The entry's name then appears in a formula score's "Add an item..." pull-down like an item. Entries apply to the whole recipe.

Testing: type example numbers in the boxes under the formula (for an entry, its best value). The check line shows the result or why it would be empty.
